# Conformal Emergent Reality Model (CERM)

### Can the universe build its own clocks and rulers?

**Research by Salman Akhtar · Independent researcher**

The Conformal Emergent Reality Model explores whether physical scale and time can arise from geometry itself—and what that could mean for matter, gravity and the life of the cosmos.

**[Explore the illustrated research hub](https://kalahari00.github.io/Conformal-Emergent-Reality-Model-CERM/)** · **[Watch the video playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLachbSm40vp8)**

## Start with the question

A clock normally sits outside the experiment it measures. But when the experiment is the whole universe, there is nowhere outside it to put that clock. CERM asks how geometry might supply both the ordering of events and the physical scale needed to measure them.

The field **Ω = φχ** combines a local geometric response with **Chronos**, accumulated curvature-weighted history. The manuscripts follow this construction into gravity, quantum evolution, particle masses, dark matter and the proposed transition between cosmic aeons.
